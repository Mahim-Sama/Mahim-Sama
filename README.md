## 🚀 What I Work On

### 🌎 World Models & Visual Representation Learning

I am currently exploring **world-model-based representation learning** for downstream perception and reasoning tasks.

My recent work investigates whether representations learned through world-model pretraining can provide useful capabilities for **object localization, target presence estimation, and instance-conditioned visual understanding**, without requiring large vision-language models or end-to-end backbone fine-tuning.

### 👁️ Computer Vision & One-Shot Object Retrieval

My recent research includes **WALDO: One-Shot Exemplar-Conditioned Object Detection in Cluttered Scenes**, a lightweight detection framework that uses frozen **V-JEPA 2.1** representations for exemplar-conditioned localization and target-presence prediction.

The work studies an important question: whether world-model representations can transfer to **fine-grained object localization and absence detection** when only a single reference exemplar and a short description are available.

WALDO uses only **3.4M trainable parameters** while keeping the V-JEPA backbone frozen. Experiments further investigate whether the benefits come from the world-model pretraining objective rather than simply from input resolution or representation scale.

🔗 **[WALDO — arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28216)**

### 🤖 Reinforcement Learning & Agentic AI

I am currently learning and exploring **reinforcement learning, agentic systems, and autonomous task execution** as the next stage of my research development.

I am particularly interested in how **RL, world models, multimodal perception, and tool-using agents** can be combined to create systems that can:

* perceive their environment,
* reason over visual and textual evidence,
* plan multi-step actions,
* use external tools and information,
* validate their own actions, and
* adapt their behavior based on feedback.

My long-term interest is in developing AI systems that move from **prediction → reasoning → action → feedback**, rather than stopping at passive inference.

### 🧠 Multimodal & Vision-Language AI

I work with vision-language models and multimodal systems, including fine-tuning domain-specific VLMs and evaluating generated outputs beyond conventional similarity-based metrics.

My work involves combining **visual evidence, natural language instructions, structured information, and learned representations** for domain-specific reasoning tasks.

### 🔗 RAG, GraphRAG & Knowledge-Enhanced AI

I have developed RAG systems and knowledge-enhanced computer vision pipelines, including **GraphRAG-based contextual reasoning** with Dockerized **FalkorDB** knowledge graphs.

My interest in this area focuses on grounding AI systems with **external knowledge, structured relations, and contextual information** rather than relying solely on parametric knowledge.

### 📊 Clustering & Unsupervised Learning

I have worked with **fuzzy c-means clustering, rule-guided clustering, and physics-guided approaches** for grouping and analyzing visual and textual data.

These projects explore how domain knowledge and explicit constraints can improve the behavior of conventional unsupervised learning methods.
