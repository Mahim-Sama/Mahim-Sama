# 👋 Hi, I'm Md. Mahfuzur Rahman

### Machine Learning Engineer | AI Researcher | Computer Vision & Generative AI

I am a **Machine Learning Engineer and AI researcher** based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a research and engineering focus on building intelligent systems that combine **computer vision, multimodal learning, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and knowledge-driven reasoning**.

My interests lie particularly in developing AI systems that can move beyond conventional pattern recognition toward **context-aware, reasoning-oriented, and domain-informed decision making**.

Currently, I work as a **Junior Software Engineer (ML Team) at Silicon Orchard LTD**, where I work on machine learning systems, RAG pipelines, computer vision, multimodal models, clustering, and knowledge-enhanced AI systems.

## 🚀 What I Work On

### 🧠 Multimodal & Vision-Language AI

I work with vision-language models and multimodal systems, including fine-tuning domain-specific VLMs and evaluating generated outputs beyond conventional similarity-based metrics.

### 👁️ Computer Vision & Remote Sensing

My work includes object detection, image understanding, disaster assessment, vehicle imagery analysis, and context-aware visual reasoning.

### 🔗 RAG, GraphRAG & Knowledge-Enhanced AI

I have developed RAG systems and knowledge-enhanced computer vision pipelines, including **GraphRAG-based contextual reasoning** with Dockerized **FalkorDB** knowledge graphs.

### 📊 Clustering & Unsupervised Learning

I have worked with fuzzy c-means clustering, rule-guided clustering, and physics-guided approaches for grouping and analyzing visual and textual data.

### 🤖 Agentic AI & Visual Reasoning

I have also worked on a training-free, one-shot object retrieval system combining visual foundation models with a **ReAct-based VLM agent** capable of multi-step visual reasoning and evidence-based re-ranking.
